# Network Planner for Copilot: privacy

Network Planner is a set of instructions and reference files that runs inside your own Microsoft 365 Copilot. It has no server of its own and makes no calls to outside services.

The device names, addresses and plans you share stay in your Copilot conversation and are handled under your organisation's Microsoft 365 terms. The author of Network Planner never receives them.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/rob-paprocki/network-planner/issues.
